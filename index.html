    Rolling it out in a new building

    1. SurveyList the rooms, their AC units and thermostats, and the socket circuits worth controlling. Mark essential circuits.
    2. Add sensorsThermal people counters in each zone, plus smart plugs or PDU relays on non-essential circuits. Most thermostats already report room temperature.
    3. Connect the gatewayPut the ThermaSense gateway on the building network. It talks BACnet/IP, Modbus TCP, MQTT or KNX to the BMS and devices.
    4. Upload floor plans & map roomsUpload each floor's plan as a PDF and drag the rooms into place. In Building setup, enter each thermostat ID, protocol and address, your working hours and your holidays. Export the config to reuse it.
    5. Shadow mode (2 weeks)The AI watches and logs what it would do while the building keeps its old schedule. Check the leaks it finds and the savings it predicts.
    6. Go liveSwitch to Live control. Wall thermostats still work as overrides, and facilities can take over any room at any time.
